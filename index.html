MODEL BOUNDARY

What is measured, and what is modeled?

The neural model does not receive chamber coordinates, object lists, target positions, or ideal actions.

The camera, captions, neural field, and history are observer-side displays and never feed back into control.

Natural

Runs the loaded CNS with a disclosed ongoing-state hypothesis, physiology, associative memory, identified leg-motor output and a six-leg planar plant. Descending activity can coordinate gait timing, but the body cannot translate without mapped leg-effector activity.

Causal

Routes embodied sensory and proprioceptive input through the graph and derives body movement only from identified neural outputs. It removes memory input and ongoing-state assumptions, so it may be quieter or stationary.

Evoked

Uses the published zero-baseline style with narrow outputs. It is for stimulation experiments and can remain still.

The wiring and neuron identities come from the loaded connectome. Membrane dynamics, receptor transduction, unresolved transmitter effects, ongoing state, memory, muscle transfer, physiology, and the planar six-leg body are models. This software cannot establish consciousness, subjective experience, or biological life.
