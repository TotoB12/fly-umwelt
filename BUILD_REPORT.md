Version 3.8 is locally coherent and statically deployable. It removes false behavioral competence from the transitional body: tonic leg-pool imbalance no longer steers, contact no longer starts hidden reverse/turn programs, and tonic readiness cannot start gait without separate coordination evidence. Unobstructed walking remains inside cited active-bout speed and joint-cycle envelopes, and contact-honest ingestion remains intact. The resulting failures of steering-DN spiking, obstacle recovery and phasic stop control are preserved rather than patched with a body controller. All 3.6/3.7 front-leg evidence and the two negative DSI findings remain unchanged.

The release also lands the staged articulated mechanics foundation: pinned controller-free NeuroMechFly v2 morphology, MuJoCo 3.9 browser WASM, a conservative motor-coordinate review and a qualification-only world-Worker boundary. The body compiles as 70 bodies, 42 position actuators, zero adhesion actuators and six contact sensors, settles deterministically and responds causally to explicit perturbations. Direct compiled geometry corrects the femur–tibia sign (`+` coordinate/torque is flexion), and a servo-disabled generalized-torque probe carries one already-qualified resolved front fast-flexor twitch into physical joint motion with exactly zero position-actuator force. A deterministic room derivative now maps spawn pose, four chamber boundaries and static wall/shelter footprints into MuJoCo using one source-matched explicit pair per collider × 55 pinned contact-body geoms; a wall intervention creates real front-tarsus contacts and enters the matching local sensors without exposing room state to the CNS. Exact physics-profile identity prevents cross-room restore. It is not the default plant and receives no live neural motor or adhesion command.

A separate restrained FlyMimic qualification now closes the existence of internal anatomy-derived front-leg routes without pretending they are a live neural body. The pinned anchored model compiles as 73 bodies, 15 one-state Hill muscles, 15 spatial tendons and 71 meshes. From identical serialized state, isolated `LFTibia_flex_93434` and `LFTibia_extensor_93932` excitation cause opposite pitch, velocity and generalized-force changes consistent with their opposite moment arms. The model is not merged with the free-root viewer: its compiled mass is `2.494271478 mg` versus `1.02431 mg`, its physiology contains source-disclosed estimates/optimization, and external contacts were omitted from source validation. Most importantly, every control clamps zero to `0.0001`; passive force is nonzero in eight keyframe muscles. The zero-evidence/BANC gate is therefore frozen red and automatic integration stays disabled.

The source failure stays frozen, while a distinct zero-safe profile now removes exactly the 15 muscle floors in memory and preserves every checked keyframe mechanical quantity. Zero-initial controls and activation stay exact zero without erasing passive force. Exact bundled BANC rows qualify LF fast flexor and FETi correspondences as identity-only; SETi remains distinct, predicted-GABA conflicts remain visible, and gain/timing stay null. A compiled reconciliation finds 64 semantic body matches but proves that mass, root topology, segment frames/segmentation, actuator semantics and contact sensors are incompatible, so it blocks merging or parameter transfer. The browser Worker qualifies only this zero-safe, still-disconnected profile.

The release is not a complete digital fly. Preparation-compatible muscle excitation gain/timing, one mechanically reconciled free-root muscle/contact body, VNC-emergent gait, full sensory periphery, heterogeneous physiology, in-circuit state/learning and multi-assay whole-organism validation all remain open.

## Scientific qualifications

- The bridge's five held-out metrics are numerical/causal implementation consequences, not independent biological animals or recordings.
- The four locomotor held-out seeds are untouched deterministic initial conditions, not independent animals; the fitting target is only the provisional motor transfer.
- Exact steering DNs and MDN-related reversal are spike-silent in the current assays; obstacle recovery and phasic bout/stop recruitment are not qualified.
- Exact phasic diagnostics find no conservatively labeled P9/BPN/oDN1/walking-DNg/halt population and zero spike frames in the present DNp09/MDN/DNp42 populations, even while the broad descending proxy reaches 2.27–2.42 Hz.
- The motor-excitability bridge compensates for missing heterogeneous motor physiology and is not a measured intracellular transfer.
- Published speed values describe active uninterrupted walking bouts, not a requirement to move continuously or a validated spontaneous chamber-average distribution.
- Raw force/FeCO traces were not acquired; no access challenge was bypassed.
- The provisional GCaMP6f layer lacks preparation-specific kinetics, baseline, expression, neuropil and driver-overlap calibration.
- Absolute flexor force is qualified only for resolved spikes under the restrained probe observation; unresolved flexors, extensors and other legs do not inherit it.
- The live planar body still has one dynamic joint per leg, a coordination-gated shared gait phase and engineering traction/contact. Contact has no behavior timer or turn selector.
- The staged articulated body uses upstream engineering passive joints, position actuators, friction, solver and contact settings. Position servos are fully disabled only in the torque probe. It has no normalized BANC-to-actuator gain or internal muscle/tendon path.
- The separate FlyMimic body has anatomy-derived restrained LF paths, but its physiological force/velocity terms include estimates and optimization, its 2.494271478 mg mass differs from the viewer body's 1.02431 mg, and its source validation omits untethered contact demand.
- Every source-profile FlyMimic actuator clamps requested zero to `0.0001`; passive force remains nonzero. The separate zero-safe profile fixes only the floor and does not validate physiological gain. Its two femur–tibia labels now have identity-only BANC correspondences, not excitation gain or timing.
- The articulated afferent vector carries physical joint/contact and FeCO evidence without world geometry, but is qualification-only. Raw contact force has no source-supported strain-afferent gain and therefore remains disabled on the CNS vector.
- Articulated room x/y footprints and spawn pose are now physical in qualification, but 5 mm extrusion, 1 mm boundary thickness, rigid/static behavior and source-matched pair values remain engineering rather than measured material parameters. Food, water, light and threat remain nonblocking.
- Tarsal adhesion is disabled. FlyGym implements it as six active normal-attraction actuators, and the official browser game times them from excluded CPG/preprogrammed stance/swing phase; neither passive stickiness nor synthetic neural timing is defensible yet.
- BANC omits the lamina, ocelli and ocellar ganglion and has bilateral antennal-nerve damage.
- Global LIF parameters, transmitter signs, background state and sensory Poisson transfers remain broad approximations.
- Nothing in this release establishes biological life, consciousness, pain, welfare or specimen continuity.

Exact zero from a zero state, two fast-unit identities and the incompatibility audit are complete; they deliberately do not solve gain, frame/centre-of-mass/inertia reconstruction or untethered contact.
